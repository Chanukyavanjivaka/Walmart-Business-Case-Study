# 🛒 Walmart Business Case Study – Retail Data Analytics using Python

## 📘 Overview
This project focuses on analyzing Walmart’s customer purchase data to uncover **actionable business insights**.  
The main goal is to understand how **Gender, Age, and Marital Status** influence customer spending behavior and how Walmart can use data-driven insights to improve its marketing and business strategy.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze customer purchase patterns based on demographics.
- Apply **statistical analysis** to check spending differences.
- Validate results using **Confidence Intervals** and **Central Limit Theorem (CLT)**.
- Provide **business recommendations** to enhance revenue and customer targeting.

---

---

## 📊 Key Insights
- **Gender Impact:** Males spend approximately ₹700 more per transaction than females.  
- **Age Group:** The **26–35 age group** has the highest purchase power.  
- **Marital Status:** No significant difference observed between married and unmarried customers.  
- **Central Limit Theorem:** Sampling distributions became normal even for skewed raw data.  
- **Confidence Intervals:** Narrower intervals for large samples ensured reliable estimates.
